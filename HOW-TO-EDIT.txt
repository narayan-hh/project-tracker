PROJECT TRACKER
===============
A local website. Nothing is online. Open index.html to start.


THE SECTIONS (left menu)
------------------------
HOME           A summary of everything - open tasks, team progress,
               upcoming reminders and total spend.
MY TASKS       Three columns: Priority 1, 2 and 3.
WEEKLY REVIEW  What moved, what slipped, who needs a conversation.
TEAM MEMBERS   A card per person, with a progress meter.
REMINDERS      Date, reminder, who, note. Overdue ones turn red.
BUDGET         Date, Expense Title, Amount, Vendor, By (M4C emp),
               Budget Head, Approved by.
SETTINGS       (bottom) Spreadsheets, backups, site details.

A red number next to Weekly Review is the count of things needing
a look right now. When it is gone, nothing is overdue or stalled.


KEYBOARD - THE FAST WAY TO WORK
-------------------------------
Ctrl+K   the jump box. Type a few letters of any task, goal, person,
         reminder or expense and press Enter to go straight there.
         It also runs actions - type "import", "csv", "reminder".
/        the same jump box
n        new item on whichever page you are on
i        import a spreadsheet into this page
g then h / t / w / m / r / b / s   go to Home, Tasks, Weekly review,
         teaM, Reminders, Budget, Settings
?        the full list of shortcuts
Esc      close whatever is open


MY TASKS
--------
Each task has a month, a name, a note, a due date and a status.
Statuses: Not started / In progress / Completed.

  - DRAG a card into another column to change its priority.
    The P1/P2/P3 dropdown still works too.
  - Due dates colour themselves: red once overdue, amber within
    three days.
  - Completed tasks drop to the bottom of their column.
  - The filter bar searches, and narrows by month, status, or
    "Overdue" only.


WEEKLY REVIEW
-------------
Nothing to fill in - it is worked out from what you have already
recorded. It gathers:

  overdue tasks, tasks due in the next 7 days, what you finished
  this week, overdue and upcoming reminders, goals marked at risk
  or on hold, people with no check-in for 14+ days, goals with no
  subtasks yet, and tasks with no due date.

"Copy this review" puts it on the clipboard as plain text, ready to
paste into an email or a message. "Print / save as PDF" gives a clean
sheet with the menu and background stripped out, for a meeting.

For "finished this week" to work, tasks need to be marked Completed
in the app - that is when the date gets recorded.


SPREADSHEETS - IMPORT AND EXPORT
--------------------------------
EVERY section has its own Import and Export buttons. You do not need
to go to Settings. Press i on any page for the same thing.

Import accepts:
  - Excel .xlsx files, straight from your computer. No conversion
    needed. If the file has several sheets you choose which one.
  - .csv and .tsv files
  - cells copied out of Excel and pasted into the box

Drag a file onto the drop area, or click to choose one.

Nothing is saved until you press the button. There are two, both
saying the same thing: one at the bottom of the window that is always
on screen, and one under the preview. It tells you what it will do -
"Save 3 rows into Budget entries".

Then, before anything is added, you get:
  - a tick box for whether the first row is column headings
  - a dropdown per field so you can correct what each column means
    (it guesses first, and is usually right)
  - a preview of the rows that will be added
  - a choice: add to what is there, or replace the whole section
  - a tick box to skip rows already in the tracker

Your sheet should be laid out the normal way: the headings across the
top row, and one record per row underneath. That is what it expects.

Headings recognised automatically include: Month, Task, Name, Title,
Status, Priority, Due date, Deadline, Date, Person, Owner, Who,
Vendor, Supplier, Paid to, By, Expense by, Amount, Budget head,
Approved by, Approver, Sanctioned by, Note, Objective, Timeline, Role.

"By" and "Approved by" are told apart correctly, so a sheet with both
columns maps the right way round.

Dates are read in any of these forms: 2026-09-05, 05/09/2026,
5-9-2026, 20-Aug-2026, and real Excel date cells. Day comes first
in the slash form.

Status words are read loosely. "done", "completed", "in progress",
"ongoing", "not started", "yet to start" are all understood.

sample-import.xlsx in this folder is a working example - two sheets,
real date cells. Try importing it to see how it behaves.

Export saves the section as a CSV that opens directly in Excel. Edit
it there and import it back if that is easier than typing.


EDITING
-------
Click almost any text and type. Editable text highlights on hover.
Press Enter to finish. Everything saves as you go.

DELETING gives you an Undo. Press the x, and a black bar appears at
the bottom with an Undo button for a few seconds. Nothing asks "are
you sure" any more except deleting a whole goal or team member, which
takes a lot of work with it.


BACKUPS - PLEASE READ
---------------------
Data is stored inside this browser, not in a file. So:
  - use the same browser every time
  - do not use a private / incognito window
  - clearing "cookies and site data" will erase everything

Settings -> Export backup saves a real .json file. Do this regularly.
Settings -> Restore backup loads it back.

If saving ever stops working, a red warning bar appears along the
bottom of the screen. If you see it, export immediately.

BUDGET - THE COLUMNS
--------------------
Date            when the money was spent
Expense Title   what it was for
Amount          numbers only; ₹ signs and commas are stripped on import
Vendor          the shop, supplier or person paid
By (M4C emp)    which Mantra4change employee spent it
Budget Head     chosen from the list at the bottom of the page
Approved by     who approved it. Left blank it shows red as
                "Not yet approved", and the tile at the top counts
                how many are still waiting, with the total amount.

Every cell is editable in place - click and type. The table scrolls
sideways on a narrow screen instead of squashing the columns.
